# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-18)

The first version published as a GitHub release, as a universal macOS zip (signed ad hoc, not
notarized).

### Added

- **Info View:** a strip along the bottom of the editor, like Live's own Info View. Point at any
  control on any of the ten pages, or in the header, and it shows what it does and what its values
  mean; elsewhere, a short hint. It replaces the tooltips.
- The window is 64 px taller to make room: 1080 × 794 by default, 960 × 704 at its smallest.
- The README's screenshot shows the editor with the Info View.

## 1.0.0

The first version: programs in Single, Double and Drums mode, two effect processors, combinations and the step arpeggiator. Not published as a release.
